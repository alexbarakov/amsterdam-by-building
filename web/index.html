    About this map

    All 192,000 buildings of Amsterdam, coloured by the year they were built and set in the history that shaped them.

    A non-commercial project, made out of curiosity and for education.

    Made by Alex Barakov.

    Inspired by Sasha Bogachev and his Paris, building by building.

    LAYRS projects

    LAYRS maps cities building by building: each one with its age and the layers of history it belongs to.

    • LAYRS: Amsterdam, you are here.
    • LAYRS: Delft: 37,000 buildings of Delft, from Vermeer's street to the 1654 gunpowder blast.
    • LAYRS: London: 213,000 buildings of inner London, from the Great Fire to the new skyline.
    • LAYRS: St. Petersburg: 107,000 buildings of St. Petersburg, from Pushkin's flats to the 1941 air-raid reports (in Russian).

    Open source

    The code and the pipeline are on GitHub. Know a piece of Amsterdam's history that lives in its buildings? Add it as a layer: a list of addresses, a short story for each and the Wikipedia article it rests on.

    Data

    Downloaded on 12 October 2026; street photos load live.

    • Buildings, roofs, heights, years: 3D BAG v2025.09.03 (TU Delft), its LoD 2.2 roofs in the centre and for listed buildings, on the Dutch building register BAG. CC BY 4.0.
    • Towers and spires the model leaves out: AHN surface model, 0.5 m, via PDOK. CC0.
    • Water, boundary, streets: BGT and BAG via PDOK and the City of Amsterdam. CC0.
    • Monuments, history layers: national monument register (RCE) and City of Amsterdam open data. CC0 or CC BY.
    • Energy labels: RVO via RIVM Atlas Leefomgeving. Public domain.
    • Names, photos, stories: Wikidata, Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons; each photo credits its author and licence.
    • Street photos: City of Amsterdam panoramas. CC BY 4.0.
    • Archive photos, 1940s–2010s: Stadsarchief Amsterdam beeldbank, linked to buildings by the Amsterdam Time Machine (University of Amsterdam). Shown from the archive's own server; each links to its record, where the rights are stated.

    Method

    Placeholder and rounded years in BAG are estimated from dated neighbours; where the monument register dates a building earlier, the register wins. Approximate years are paler and marked “c.”. Every hand-written story is checked against its Wikipedia article; the residents layer is found automatically and may contain errors.

    Methode

    Plaatshouders en afgeronde jaartallen in de BAG zijn geschat uit gedateerde buren; waar het monumentenregister een gebouw eerder dateert, wint het register. Geschatte jaren zijn lichter en gemarkeerd met “c.”. Elk zelfgeschreven verhaal is gecontroleerd aan zijn Wikipedia-artikel; de laag met bewoners is automatisch gevonden en kan fouten bevatten.
